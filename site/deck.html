Bearing Health Monitor1

Bearings fail slowly, then all at onceAnd most plants find out at the second stage

Rolling-element bearings degrade progressively — microscopic spalling → characteristic defect frequencies → heat → seizure. The failure is detectable in advance from vibration.

Yet mid-tier and legacy plants still run reactive or fixed-interval maintenance: replace on a calendar, or replace after it breaks.

A single unplanned stop on a critical line — a mill stand, a main pump, a furnace blower — costs tens of thousands of dollars per hour, plus collateral damage and safety exposure.

Why the gap persists

  • Premium monitoring platforms are priced and contracted for tier-1 plants.
  • Route-based manual measurement is quarterly at best — faults develop between rounds.
  • Systems that need historical training data are useless on day one.
  • Foreign vendors sell in English, invoice in EUR, and support from another time zone.
The problem2

Physics, not a black boxExplainable on day one, with zero training data

Bearing geometry and shaft speed determine four characteristic defect frequencies exactly (BPFO / BPFI / BSF / FTF). A defect on a given element rings at its frequency and harmonics. Find the comb, name the element.

Evidence5

What this evidence does not supportStated here because you would find it in the repo anyway

No false-alarm rate

One healthy record, reading 3.95 against a warn threshold of 4.0 — a 1.2% margin. A rate needs a healthy population.

No lead-time distribution

n = 1 failure event. One observation has no dispersion, so 2.5 days has no lower bound — and nothing supports the word weeks.

Ball faults unsolved

Detected as a level change, but the element ranking is wrong. Race faults — the majority of failures — are solid.

Nothing through the product's sensor chain

CWRU and IMS are lab piezo recordings at constant speed and load. The product is a MEMS part on a magnet mount, duty-cycled, on a variable-load machine. That gap is unmeasured — closing it is the next milestone.

Closing this is cheap and mostly needs no hardware

IMS sets 1 and 3 are already downloaded (+3 failures). XJTU-SY, PRONOSTIA/FEMTO, Paderborn and MFPT take the run-to-failure count from 1 to ~35 and build a real healthy denominator. Weeks of work, zero capex.

Anchor region. The Karaganda industrial basin is Kazakhstan's heavy-industry core: integrated steel, coal mines, iron-ore and coal-prep plants — thousands of monitored points of rotating equipment in non-explosive areas alone.

Expand. Kazakhmys, Kazzinc, ERG, cement plants, power stations, oil & gas processing.

The budget is proven — locally

In March 2026 Qarmet (Temirtau) commissioned SPM Instrument's Intellinova Parallel EN online monitoring plus RDI Iris M, announced as an industry first for the region.

We read that as validation, not as a closed door. A tier-1 plant just proved that predictive-maintenance budget exists in this basin and that the buyer is sophisticated.

Our wedge is the tier below it: mid-market and legacy plants that will not sign a six-figure foreign platform contract, and the un-instrumented balance of plant at the large sites.

The moat is not the algorithm

Envelope analysis is public-domain literature (Balderston 1969 → McFadden & Smith 1984 → Antoni 2007), and the patent that inspired this project expired in 2013. What compounds is local presence, per-plant failure data, and the relationships — not the DSP. We would rather state that than pretend otherwise.

Go to market9

What could kill thisAnd what we are doing about each

RiskMitigation
The customer cannot act on the alert — no spare, no planner, no crew, so a warning creates no capturable value. This is the first thing stage 1 tests, before any capex. If true, the business is diagnosis + parts + repair, not a sensor — and we would rather learn that for $700 than for $178k.
Supply chain — Digi-Key and Mouser will not ship to Kazakhstan; PJRC/SparkFun bar Teensy export. Documented working routes (local importer, TME Poland, Chip One Stop). Stage 1 and 2 need no restricted parts at all; the exposure only begins at stage 3.
Solo founder across embedded, DSP, cloud, sales and certification. Actively seeking a commercial co-founder with plant relationships. This is the constraint we rate highest.
False alarms destroy trust — one bad page can end a pilot. Sustained-crossing logic (3 consecutive captures) already ships. The false-alarm denominator is the top engineering priority, and it is cheap to build.
Incumbents bundle monitoring free to sell bearings and lubricants. We do not compete on unit price with a loss-leader. We compete on being here, in Russian and Kazakh, on legacy equipment the OEM does not care about.
